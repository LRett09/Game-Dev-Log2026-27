    ⚙ Module Bay

    Swap between the modules you own. From 1,000m you can also upgrade them to Mk II and Mk III, which makes their good effects stronger (penalties stay the same). Upgrades reset when you ascend.

    🔨 Workshop

    Your stackable upgrades. They can only be bought from shops you come across while drilling, and they reset when you ascend. Tap a ☆ to favorite one upgrade so shops offer it more often, or 🚫 to banish up to two so shops offer them much less.

    📋 Patch Notes

    ⛏️ About The Deep Mine

    Drill deep, but be warned: the lower you go the more dangerous the caves get. Upgrade your drill with new skills, modules, and research permanent upgrades. Can you reach the bottom?

    Skills and Ascension are on the left edge of the screen. Module Bay, Workshop and Challenges are on the right.

    The game is frozen until you choose.

    🏪 Wandering Shop

    You have come across a shop deep in the rock. Power, oxygen, heat and every timer are frozen while you are here.

    Each item can be bought once. When you leave, the shop stays behind you, so choose carefully! (If a Surface Sample ever pulls you back above it, you can return.)
